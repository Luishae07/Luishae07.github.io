FAQ

Common questions about Luis bot.

How do I add it?

Click "Add to Discord", pick your server, and authorize. The bot registers all 60 commands instantly.

Does it need permissions?

Moderation commands check the caller's permissions. The bot ships with Administrator so it can run everything.

What powers the AI?

DeepSeek V4 Flash, used for the /ask command, AI-generated polls, and auto-replies in a dedicated channel.

What else can it do?

Weather, crypto prices, memes, trivia, QR codes, URL shortener, polls, timers, and full moderation tools.
